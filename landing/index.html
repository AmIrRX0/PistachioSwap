Built around the moment you press sign

Most losses in a swap interface happen in the last two seconds — the wrong spender, an allowance that never expires, a token whose name is not the token you think it is. Those are the parts we work on hardest.

Every transaction, in full

The review screen names the destination, the spender, the exact amount and the calldata before you approve. Signed transactions are re-parsed and checked against what you reviewed before they are broadcast.

Routes compared on net output

Quotes from several aggregators and bridges are compared after network cost, not on the headline number. You see the minimum you will receive and the maximum you will pay before committing.

Unknown tokens stay unknown

Token names and symbols come from arbitrary contracts, so a symbol that uses lookalike characters is never rendered as if it were the real thing. Unverified holdings are separated from the rest and priced conservatively.

Gas Assist: tokens you cannot move

A wallet can hold valuable BEP-20 tokens and still be stuck, because paying for gas needs BNB it does not have. Gas Assist exists for exactly that failure.

  1. The package is prepared For an eligible prepaid swap the backend builds an exact three-transaction package: the fee payment, the exact token approval, and the exact swap.
  2. You review it once All three are shown together, with the exact fee and the minimum output, before a single signature is requested.
  3. One authorization signs all three With Pistachio Wallet the package is authorized through one review and one fresh passkey ceremony. Each transaction is signed locally and validated after signing.
  4. Partial packages are never submitted Any mismatch — a changed account, nonce, chain, expiry or rewritten transaction — aborts the whole package.

Gas Assist is not described as free

The network cost, the sponsorship cost and PistachioSwap fees are disclosed by the quote or the review flow before you sign anything.

Availability depends on token eligibility, provider support, liquidity, policy limits and backend configuration. When a route is not eligible the interface says so rather than failing silently.

Twenty-five curated EVM networks

Bring your own wallet — or use the one in your browser

Any standard EVM wallet connects the usual way. Pistachio Wallet is optional.

Keys never leave the worker

Pistachio Wallet holds its private key inside a dedicated worker and wipes it on lock. Vaults are stored as AES-256-GCM ciphertext, keyed from a passkey, and bound to this site's origin.

A fresh check for every sensitive action

Signing, revealing a recovery phrase and exporting a key each require a new passkey ceremony. The wallet re-verifies the account and chain after every asynchronous step and fails closed on a mismatch.

Recovery stays in your hands

Export an encrypted backup or a recovery phrase whenever you want. Removing a wallet from this browser does not delete it from any network.

Questions

Does Pistachio Swap hold my funds?

No. Pistachio Swap is a self-custodial interface. Tokens stay in your own wallet, and nothing moves until you approve and sign a transaction that the review screen has shown you in full.

What is Gas Assist?

A wallet can hold valuable BEP-20 tokens while having no BNB to pay network gas. Gas Assist is built for that case: the backend prepares an exact three-transaction package covering the fee payment, the token approval, and the swap itself.

Gas Assist is not free. The network cost, the sponsorship cost and PistachioSwap fees are disclosed in the quote or review before you sign.

Which networks are supported?

Twenty-five curated EVM networks, listed above. Gas Assist itself runs on BNB Smart Chain.

Do I have to use Pistachio Wallet?

No. Any standard EVM wallet connects through the usual wallet flow. Pistachio Wallet is an optional passkey-protected wallet held in your own browser; its private key never leaves the worker that holds it, and every sensitive action requires a fresh passkey check.

Has Pistachio Swap been audited?

Not yet. Pistachio Swap is pre-release software and has not been independently audited. Use a separate wallet and small amounts while development continues.

How are swap routes chosen?

Same-chain quotes are compared across several aggregators, including 0x, KyberSwap and Uniswap, and the comparison accounts for network cost rather than headline output alone.

Cross-chain routes are quoted from Across, deBridge DLN, Relay, Chainflip and 0x, and the route that nets out best is selected.

Before you use it

Pistachio Swap is pre-release software. It has not been independently audited and should not be treated as production-ready financial infrastructure. Swapping tokens carries risk, including permanent loss. Use separate wallets and small amounts while development continues.
